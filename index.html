The Movement

Who are we?

Artistic Humans is a Mumbai-based creative and social-impact organisation using theatre, education, and cultural platforms to turn ideas into public value.

From producing award-winning original plays and curating recurring platforms to conducting civic awareness street theatre across Maharashtra, our practice connects artistic excellence with community participation.

Stage Milestones

Our Productions & Milestones

2017

'Yugpurush'

Darshan portrayed Mahatma Gandhi at age 21. Multilingual theatrical masterpiece crossed 1,000+ international shows worldwide.

2018

'The Artist'

Original production won 10 accolades across India, including Best Play at two National Theatre Festivals and international selection.

2020

'K Se Kalakaar'

Our first 4-day festival at Poinsur Gymkhana, staging 5 original productions combining collective exchange and audience engagement.

2021

'Bhumika: The Journey'

Award-winning Hindi & Gujarati production. Winner of 18 accolades across two National festivals and 4 Maharashtra State awards.

2023

'Amrit Mahotsav 75×75×75'

75 shows in 75 institutions in 1 day on Shaheed Day (March 23, 2023). Impacted 1,000+ students and 18,000+ citizens with Maharashtra Govt.

2023

'Pruthvi Che Shetkari'

World's first dance-drama play based on millets, featured internationally by the UN Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO).

2024

'Sanskrutik Kala Manch'

A recurring performing arts stage co-founded with Poinsur Gymkhana. Hosted 600+ artists across 25 programmes over 2.5 years.

2025–26

'Rashtragranth'

Presented as India's first play based on the Constitution of India. 40+ artists & technicians translating constitutional literacy into gripping theatre.
